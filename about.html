Clear Windows & Doors is a proud local Canadian business, founded by four people with a shared vision to change the windows and doors industry for the better.

Our mission

Comfort all year round.

Our mission is simple: to help our neighbours live in comfort year-round with the most energy-efficient windows and doors available, keeping homes warm in the winter and cool in the summer.

We're committed to delivering high-quality products and services to our community, and to using the latest technology in every installation.

When you choose Clear Windows & Doors, you're not just upgrading your home. You're supporting a local business that cares about your community.
