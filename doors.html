Questions

Door FAQ.

What's the difference between XO and OXXO patio doors?

The letters describe the panels as you look from outside: X slides and O is fixed. An XO door has one sliding and one fixed panel; an OXXO has four panels with the two in the middle sliding.

Are your doors energy efficient?

Our doors are available double or triple glazed with Low-E glass, and we seal them properly on installation so they keep drafts out.

Do patio doors come with screens?

Yes. Built-in screens are standard with our doors and windows.

Can you replace just the door and keep the frame?

Sometimes. It depends on the condition of the frame and the new door. We'll check during the consultation and recommend the option that will last.

Do you install the handles and locks?

Yes. We fit and adjust the hardware and seal the door as part of the installation.
