Who we are

A local team that cares how your home feels.

Clear Windows & Doors is a proud local Canadian business, founded by four people with a shared vision: better windows and doors, installed properly.

We supply and install vinyl and uPVC windows, doors and screens for homeowners across Langley, Surrey, Abbotsford, Burnaby, New Westminster and the rest of Metro Vancouver. Some customers are replacing drafty old windows one room at a time. Others are renovating, building new, or finally fixing a patio door that never sealed.

The right windows and doors make a real difference. They keep heat in during the winter and out in the summer, cut down outside noise, let in more light and make your home more secure. Our mission is simple: to help our neighbours live in comfort year-round with the most energy-efficient solutions available.

What we do

Windows, doors and the work that goes with them.

From the first measurement to the last bead of sealant, here's how we can help.

01 · Residential windows

Windows made to fit your home

Casement, sliding, tilt & turn, awning, picture, bay and bow, double-hung and skylights, in vinyl and uPVC. We help you choose the style that suits each room and how you use it.

  • ENERGY STAR certified windows
  • Low-E glass as standard
  • Double or triple glazing
Learn about residential windows
02 · Entry doors

A front door that seals properly

Entrance, back-entrance, stable, French and composite doors, chosen for security, insulation and the look of your home.

  • Double or triple glazed options
  • Weather-sealed on installation
  • Hardware fitted and adjusted
Learn about entry doors
03 · Patio & sliding doors

More light, easier access

Vinyl sliding patio doors in two-panel (XO) and four-panel (OXXO) layouts, plus bi-folding doors that open up almost the whole wall.

  • Large glass panels
  • Smooth sliding operation
  • Built-in screens included
Learn about patio & sliding doors
04 · Window & door installation

Removal, installation and cleanup

We measure, remove the old units, install the new ones with weatherproof membranes and sealing, finish inside and out, and clean up before we leave.

  • Free in-home or virtual consultation
  • Interior and exterior finishing
  • Old units taken away
Learn about window & door installation

05 · Window screens

Fresh air without the bugs

Built-in screens come standard with our windows and doors. We also make custom-sized replacement screens, including pet-safe, UV-resistant and retractable options.

  • Custom sizes
  • Pet-safe and anti-mosquito mesh
  • Colour options to match frames
Learn about window screens
06 · Repairs & replacement

Fixing leaks, drafts and worn seals

Not every problem needs a new window. We troubleshoot leaks, drafts, fogged or broken seals and worn hardware, and tell you honestly when replacing makes more sense.

  • Leaks and drafts
  • Seals and weatherstripping
  • Hardware and screens
