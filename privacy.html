Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 2026

Draft for review. Written from what this website actually collects. Please review it before the site goes live.

Clear Windows & Doors ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and when you deal with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We follow British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

  • Quote and contact requests: your name, phone number, email, project location, details about your project, measurements, photos you choose to upload, and your preferred contact method and timing.
  • When we work with you: measurements, photos of the openings, product choices, quotes, contracts, invoices and payment records.
  • Basic website data: your cookie choice, stored in your own browser.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and book your consultation.
  • To prepare quotes, order products, schedule and carry out your installation, and provide warranty service and support.
  • To keep the business records we're required to keep, such as invoices.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only with people who help us do the work, such as product suppliers that need measurements to make your order, and service providers that host our website and records. They may only use it for that purpose. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Cookies

This site uses only the storage it needs to work, such as remembering your cookie choice. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add website analytics in the future, they'll run only if you choose "Accept all". You can change your choice any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests that don't go ahead for up to two years, and records of completed jobs for as long as we need them for warranty, tax and legal reasons. After that we delete or destroy them securely.

Keeping it safe

Only staff who need your information can see it, and we use secure hosting.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent (unless we must keep it by law). Email sales@clearwindowsanddoors.com or call (236) 989-8351.
