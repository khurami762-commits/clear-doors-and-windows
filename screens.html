Questions

Screen FAQ.

Do new windows and doors come with screens?

Yes. Built-in screens are standard with all of our windows and doors.

Can you replace a damaged or missing screen?

Yes. We make custom-sized replacement screens to fit your windows and doors.

Which mesh is best if I have pets?

Pet-safe mesh is tougher and stands up to paws and claws much better than standard mesh.

Can screens match my window frames?

Yes. Screen frames come in colour options to match your windows and doors.
