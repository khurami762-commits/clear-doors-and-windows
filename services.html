Window & door installation

Installed with care, from start to finish.

Good installation matters as much as a good product. Here's what every installation includes.

01

Consultation

Free, in person or virtual. We measure every opening, explain the options and send a written quote.

02

Removal

We carefully remove the old windows or doors and protect your floors and furniture.

03

Installation

New units are set level and square, with weatherproof membranes and sealing, then finished inside and out.

04

Cleanup

We take the old units away, clean up and walk you through how everything works.

New builds & renovations

Working alongside your build.

Building new or renovating? We can work to your schedule, supply and install windows and doors once the framing and wrap are ready, and coordinate with your contractor.

Repairs & replacement

Not every problem needs a new window.

We troubleshoot the problem first and tell you honestly whether a repair will do or a replacement makes more sense.

Leaks & drafts

Finding where water or air gets in and sealing it properly.

Fogged or broken seals

Glass units that have lost their seal and fog up between the panes.

Sticking windows & doors

Sashes and sliders that are hard to open, close or lock.

Hardware

Worn handles, locks, hinges and rollers.

Screens

Torn, bent or missing screens, made to measure.

Maintenance

Check-ups that keep windows and doors sealing and working properly.

What to expect

Clear quotes, no pressure.

After we measure, you get a written quote that lists every product, the glass and options, and the installation work. Take your time.

Every installation comes with a written warranty, and we offer special installation pricing for non-profits, students and military members.

Questions

Service FAQ.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Consultations and quotes are free, in person or virtual. We measure, talk through your options and send you a written quote.

Which areas do you serve?

Langley, Surrey, Abbotsford, Burnaby, New Westminster and the rest of Metro Vancouver. If you're a little further out, give us a call.

Do you take away the old windows and doors?

Yes. Removal of the old units and cleanup are part of our installation service.

Can you repair a window instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. We troubleshoot leaks, drafts and worn seals and will tell you honestly whether a repair will do.

What about warranty?

Every installation comes with a written warranty. Ask us and we'll walk you through exactly what's covered for your products.

Do you offer any special pricing?

Yes. We offer special installation pricing for non-profits, students and military members. Mention it when you ask for a quote.

What happens after I send a quote request?

We get back to you by your preferred contact method to ask any questions and book a time to measure. Then we send a written quote. There's no obligation.
