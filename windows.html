Window styles

Find the right style for each room.

Not sure which style fits? We'll recommend options for each room during your free consultation.

Casement

Hinged at the side and cranks outward. Seals tightly when closed and opens fully for airflow.

Sliding

One sash glides past the other. Simple to use and good for wide openings.

Tilt & turn

Tilts in at the top for ventilation or swings in like a door for cleaning and fresh air.

Awning

Hinged at the top and opens outward, so it can stay open in light rain.

Fixed / picture

Doesn't open. Lets in the most light and gives an unbroken view.

Bay & bow

Several windows angled out from the wall to add light and a little extra space.

Double-hung

Both sashes slide up and down, a classic look for many homes.

Skylight

Brings daylight in through the roof to rooms that need it.

Materials & frames

Vinyl, PVC and uPVC.

All three are low-maintenance and never need painting. Here's how they differ.

MaterialWhat it isWhy people choose it
VinylThe everyday name for PVC-based window frames.Affordable, insulates well, easy to look after.
PVCPolyvinyl chloride, with plasticizers that make it more flexible.Versatile and lightweight.
uPVCUnplasticized PVC: no plasticizers, so it's rigid and strong.Holds its shape, stands up to weather, suits larger units.

Glass & energy efficiency

Glass that works for you.

Old, drafty windows let heat escape all winter. Our windows are ENERGY STAR certified, and the glass you choose makes a real difference to comfort and noise.

01

Low-E glass

Standard on every window. A thin coating reflects heat back into the room in winter and keeps summer heat out.

02

Double or triple glazing

Two or three panes with insulating space between them. Triple glazing adds insulation and quiet.

03

Soundproofing

Glass options that cut down traffic and street noise.

04

Impact-resistant & fire-rated

Tougher glass for security and safety, and fire-rated options where they're needed.

Maintenance

Simple care, long life.

Vinyl and uPVC windows need very little looking after. A few minutes a year keeps them working well.

Clean the frames

Wipe vinyl and uPVC frames with mild soap and water. Avoid harsh solvents and abrasive pads.

Keep tracks clear

Vacuum dirt out of sliding tracks and sills so sashes move smoothly and drain properly.

Check the seals

Look over weatherstripping once a year. Damaged seals let in drafts and water.

Care for hardware

A little silicone lubricant keeps cranks, hinges and locks working smoothly.

Installation

Installed properly, sealed properly.

A great window can still leak if it isn't installed well. We remove the old unit carefully, check the opening is level, square and solid, then set the new window with weatherproof membranes and sealing.

Questions

Window FAQ.

Which window style is right for my home?

It depends on the room, the size of the opening and how you want it to open. Casement and awning windows seal tightly and catch breezes, sliders suit wide openings, and picture windows give the biggest view. We'll recommend options for each room during your free consultation.

What's the difference between double and triple glazing?

Double glazing has two panes of glass with an insulating space between them; triple glazing has three. Triple glazing adds insulation and can reduce outside noise. Low-E glass is standard on our windows either way.

Are your windows energy efficient?

Yes. Our windows are ENERGY STAR certified and come with Low-E glass as standard. Ask us for the ratings of the specific windows in your quote.

Do the windows come with screens?

Yes. Built-in screens are standard with all of our windows.

Do you remove the old windows?

Yes. Removal of the old windows and cleanup are part of our installation service.

How long does installation take?

It depends on how many windows you're replacing and the condition of the openings. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your quote.
